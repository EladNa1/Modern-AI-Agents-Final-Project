How it works

A Reflection Agent — grade, then self-critique

CheckMate grades each question first, then critiques its own grade against the retrieved course evidence and revises until it's confident. Every step is logged in the steps[] trace above.

1Parser

Reads every page with vision OCR (gpt-5.4-mini) and splits the exam into questions — faithfully transcribing the student's own work.

2Retriever

Pinecone vector RAG: pulls the matching official solution and the most relevant course-notes chunks to ground the grade.

3Grader

Scores the student's actual method with partial credit and written feedback — persona · chain-of-thought · few-shot · structured output.

4Reflector

Critiques its own grade against the evidence and revises, up to N passes — then approves, or escalates to a teacher.
